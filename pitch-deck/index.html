04 • Business Model • Ghana Pricing

Simple pricing.
Customer pays delivery fee, WDS keeps 20%.

Formula (Editable by Admin)
Total = Base GHS 15 + Distance*2.5 + PackageFee + Express GHS 8
Document: GHS 3
Food: GHS 5
Medicine: GHS 6
Parcel/Grocery/Other: GHS 7
Express (35-50m): +GHS 8
Commission: 20% WDS / 80% Rider
